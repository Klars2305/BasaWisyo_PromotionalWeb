Every reader deserves a way forward.

BasaWisyo originated as a student startup concept addressing an undeniable reality across secondary schools: reading readiness varies significantly within a single classroom, while teachers navigate heavy administrative demands.

“When instructional passages meet students right where they are, reading shifts from anxiety to genuine self-confidence.”

Background

Groundwork for meaningful literacy

We envision bite-sized, level-aware reading challenges that build comprehension incrementally. Materials stay accessible regardless of school connectivity constraints.

Mission

Make practice feel possible

Provide Grade 7–12 learners with approachable bilingual exercises, while equipping teachers with timely diagnostic data to guide targeted interventions.

Vision

More confident readers nationwide

A collaborative classroom environment where no reader is left behind, every achievement is celebrated, and literacy thrives in English and Filipino.

Reading support should meet students where they are.

One-size-fits-all reading assignments often leave struggling readers overwhelmed and advanced readers unengaged. BasaWisyo proposes a guided three-step loop that turns diagnostic insight into daily growth.

Six purposeful ideas. One reading journey.

Every proposed capability targets a real classroom challenge, from initial diagnostic baseline to community reading quests and teacher analytics.

01 / Start

Phil-IRI-inspired placement

A proposed initial assessment informed by the Philippine Informal Reading Inventory framework to match learners with appropriate reading levels. No official endorsement is claimed.

02 / Build

Personalized practice

Leveled reading passages and comprehension questions in both English and Filipino, designed for steady fluency and positive reinforcement.

EN / FIL
03 / Together

Bayanihan Class Quests

Cooperative reading activities where learners pool their reading milestones toward communal classroom goals, encouraging peer encouragement over competition.

04 / Share

BasaMesh offline sharing

A conceptual local-network distribution idea enabling teachers to transmit lessons and sync activity data across devices without active internet connections.

05 / Organize

DepEd reports & Form 14

Proposed automated reporting layouts formatted to simplify reading progress documentation for school records. No official DepEd integration is implied.

06 / Guide

Teacher dashboard

A proposed unified overview of class skill distributions, reading velocity, and focus areas to help educators make targeted instructional decisions.

A reading lesson should travel beyond the signal.

Many Philippine schools operate in areas with fluctuating or absent mobile connectivity. BasaMesh explores a local peer-to-peer distribution model where one teacher station shares curated reading modules directly with nearby learner tablets.

Honest concept: Proposed offline architecture for future prototype validation; not currently a live peer-to-peer protocol.
